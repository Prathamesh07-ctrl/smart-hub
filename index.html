Building Smarter Homes for Tomorrow.

SmartHub was founded with a simple vision—to make modern living more intelligent, secure, and effortless.

Founded by Devendra Tiwari, SmartHub combines innovation, technology, and thoughtful design to create smart home solutions that improve everyday life. Every product is built with reliability, elegance, and user experience at its core, helping homeowners embrace the future with confidence.
